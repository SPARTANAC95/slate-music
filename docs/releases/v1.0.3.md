# Slate Music 1.0.3

Bring your Spotify playlists home, find anything with a right-click, and let the music stop when you fall asleep.

Download **Slate-Music_1.0.3_x64-setup.exe** for Windows 10/11 x64. Existing installations can download the signed update from Settings. Installation requires confirmation while playback is paused. Your database, playlists, favorites and Spotify connection are retained.

## What's new

- **Spotify playlists instead of albums.** Pick one of your playlists, your **Liked Songs** or your **top songs** (this month, last 6 months or last year). Slate Music matches each song to the files you own and keeps Spotify's order. Liked Songs can also heart every song you own. Imported playlists update themselves each time the app opens and keep the matches you confirmed. Spotify only shares playlists you created or collaborate on. Existing connections need one **Reconnect** for the new permissions.
- **Right-click menus.** Play next, add to queue, add to a playlist, go to the album or artist, favorite, or show the file in File Explorer, for songs and albums.
- **A better queue.** Play next, clear everything after the current song, and save the queue as a playlist.
- **Sleep timer.** 15 minutes to 1½ hours, or the end of the current song.
- **Taskbar controls.** Hover over Slate Music's taskbar button for Previous, Play/Pause, Next and Favorite.
- **Original release years (optional).** A new Year column shows when each song first came out. Turn on the MusicBrainz lookup in Settings for compilations and remasters, whose files only carry the album's year.
- **Moved songs are recognized.** Reorganizing your folders no longer leaves "Unavailable" copies behind. Favorites, play counts and playlist places move with the song. Settings can remove songs that really are gone and asks before it does.
- **More covers found,** including images named after the album and covers above "CD1"/"Disc 2" folders.
- **Smoother everyday use.** Back returns to where you were. Keyboard shortcuts keep working after using a slider. Mute remembers your volume. A double-click plays a song once. Messages can be dismissed. Adding a song twice to a playlist is prevented. A system tray icon offers playback controls.

## Verification

29 TypeScript tests, 37 native Rust tests and 22 native interface scenarios passed. Focused native checks covered Spotify imports, automatic updates, moved songs, covers, removal of unavailable songs, the taskbar buttons and a live MusicBrainz lookup. Spotify reads were tested with simulated Spotify responses and a local test server. See the [validation report](https://github.com/SPARTANAC95/slate-music/blob/main/docs/VALIDATION.md).

Updater signatures are configured; a Windows Authenticode publisher certificate is not. Windows may therefore show an unknown-publisher warning. Audio uses the default Windows device at 48 kHz stereo. Opus, WMA, APE and protected files are unsupported.
