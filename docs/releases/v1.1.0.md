# Slate Music 1.1.0

Lyrics that follow the song, sound you can verify down to the last bit, and a year of your listening, all on your own PC.

Download **Slate-Music_1.1.0_x64-setup.exe** for Windows 10/11 x64. Existing installations can download the signed update from Settings. Installation requires confirmation while playback is paused. Your database, playlists, favorites and Spotify connection are retained.

## What's new

- **Now Playing.** A full-window view with a large, sharp cover, colours taken from it, and **synced lyrics** that follow the song: from an .lrc file beside it, the file's own tags or, if you switch it on, LRCLIB. Click a line to jump to it. Ctrl+L opens it.
- **Bit-perfect exclusive mode.** Settings → Playback can give Slate Music the output device to itself and send each song at its own sample rate, untouched. Now Playing's **signal path** shows every step and says when the device receives exactly what is in the file.
- **Loudness levelling, smart crossfade and an equalizer.** Every song is measured once (EBU R128, or its ReplayGain tags) so volume stays even without clipping; crossfades skip silence and never fade within an album; a 10-band equalizer is remembered for each output device.
- **Choose where music plays,** or follow Windows: plugging headphones in or reconnecting them moves the music to them.
- **Command bar (Ctrl+K)** and better search that forgives typos.
- **Home shelves** (Jump back in, Recently added, On this day, Forgotten favourites), **smart playlists** built from rules, and **Your year**: plays, minutes, streaks and your top songs, artists and albums.
- **Pick several songs, drag and drop,** sortable columns, and lists that keep their place.
- **Artist photos and bios** from MusicBrainz, Wikidata and Wikipedia (optional).
- **Last.fm scrobbling** with your own API account, and a **Discord status** ("Listening to Slate Music") that works with nothing to set up. Both stay off until you switch them on.

## Fixes

- No more silence after a long pause or after reconnecting headphones: the music follows the device Windows uses.
- Left and right channels can no longer swap after a seek or a crossfade.
- Editing the queue in a song's last moments no longer stops playback.
- Favorites set in the mini-player show in the main window at once, and the other way round.
- Spotify sign-in no longer fails when the browser opens an extra connection.
- Every finding of the full pre-release review was fixed; see the changelog.

## Verification

All 33 findings of a full review of the 1.1.0 changes were fixed. 54 TypeScript tests and the native Rust tests (including the engine's gapless, crossfade, channel-order, exclusive-mode and device-switching logic) passed, and GitHub Actions built, tested and signed this installer on Windows. See the [validation report](https://github.com/SPARTANAC95/slate-music/blob/main/docs/VALIDATION.md).

Updater signatures are configured; a Windows Authenticode publisher certificate is not. Windows may therefore show an unknown-publisher warning. Opus, WMA, APE and protected files are unsupported.
