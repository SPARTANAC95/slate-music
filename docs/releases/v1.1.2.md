# Slate Music 1.1.2

Album covers on Discord, closer lyric timing, and a lyrics view with only lyrics.

Download **Slate-Music_1.1.2_x64-setup.exe** for Windows 10/11 x64. Existing installations can download the signed update from Settings. Installation requires confirmation while playback is paused. Your database, playlists, favorites and Spotify connection are retained.

## What's new

- **Album covers on Discord.** The Discord status shows the album's cover beside the song, with the title, the artist and the album on lines of their own. Discord can only show pictures that are on the web, so the cover is found on MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive (the album and artist name are sent) rather than taken from your files. It can differ from yours, and albums that aren't found show the Slate Music icon. **Show the album cover** in Settings → Discord status switches the lookup off.
- **Closer word timing.** Lyrics that only time each line, which is what LRCLIB provides, have the words inside a line estimated. The estimate now follows how fast each song is actually sung, ends a line a little before the next one starts, and gives longer words and the held last word more time.
- **Word by word is your choice.** Lyrics options has **Word by word: On, Exact only, Off**. Exact only lights word by word where the lyric source timed each word and lights other lines as a whole; Off lights every line as a whole.
- **A lyrics view with only lyrics.** The caption, the timing buttons and the note under the lyrics are gone from the view. One small button at the top right opens **Lyrics options** with the song's timing and source, the timing adjustment and the word-by-word choice.
- **See which version you have.** Settings → Updates shows the installed version.

## Verification

81 TypeScript tests, 106 native Rust tests and 30 browser checks of the real components passed. A build of this version was run as a native Windows app on a disposable profile with generated silent songs: all 28 interface scenarios and the 6 native lyric checks passed, Settings showed the version, and the word-by-word choice was exercised in the running app. The Discord status was followed end to end against a stand-in for the Discord app: nothing was sent while the status was off; a song on a known album was shown at once and again with its Cover Art Archive cover a moment later; an album that is not online showed the Slate Music icon; pausing cleared the status; and with covers switched off no cover was sent. After a restart the library and the paused session were back. GitHub Actions built, tested and signed this installer on Windows. See the [validation report](https://github.com/SPARTANAC95/slate-music/blob/main/docs/VALIDATION.md).

Word times inside line-timed lyrics remain an estimate: against 79 songs in four languages whose words were timed by hand, words land 0.33 s from where they are sung on average (0.41 s before) and 66% are within 0.3 s (60% before). Only lyrics with their own word times (Enhanced LRC) are exact. What the app sends to Discord was checked against a stand-in; how a real Discord client draws the cover was not checked before release. Updater signatures are configured; a Windows Authenticode publisher certificate is not, so Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning. Opus, WMA, APE and protected files are unsupported.
