# Slate Music 1.1.1

Lyrics that light up word by word, in time with what you hear.

Download **Slate-Music_1.1.1_x64-setup.exe** for Windows 10/11 x64. Existing installations can download the signed update from Settings. Installation requires confirmation while playback is paused. Your database, playlists, favorites and Spotify connection are retained.

## What's new

- **Word-by-word lyrics on every synced song.** Each word fills from the left as it is sung, and the line being sung is large and sharp while the others step back. Lyrics that carry their own word times (Enhanced LRC) are followed exactly. Lyrics that only time each line, which is what LRCLIB provides, pace the words evenly inside the line's real start and end; the caption above the lyrics says which you are getting.
- **In time with what you hear.** The lyric clock runs smoothly between the engine's updates instead of restarting from each one, and trails the engine by the delay Windows reports for your output device.
- **Adjust by ear.** **−** and **+** above the lyrics move this song's lyrics later or earlier in tenths of a second, remembered for the song. **Lyrics delay** in Settings → Playback is for speakers and headphones that play late (Bluetooth and other wireless), remembered for each device.
- **Pauses in the singing** show three dots that fill as the pause passes, including a lead-in before a late first line.
- **Lyrics with a translation** (two lines sharing a time stamp) are lit and centred together.
- **More lyrics found on your PC:** the fields Mp3tag, foobar2000 and MusicBee use in FLAC and Ogg files, and .lrc files saved as UTF-16.

## Fixes

- After a pause or a seek, the player could flick back for a moment when an older update arrived late.
- Seeking to a lyric line while paused could highlight the line before it.
- After clicking the seek bar, Left and Right moved the song a tenth of a second instead of skipping five seconds.
- Shuffle from the command bar or an artist's page always started with the list's first song.
- At small window sizes the lyrics list could stop following the window's height.
- The command bar searched the library again several times a second while music played.
- The listening session was written to disk every two seconds even when nothing had changed.

## Verification

80 TypeScript tests, 103 native Rust tests and 26 browser checks of the real components passed. A build of this version was run as a native Windows app on a disposable profile with generated silent songs: all 28 interface scenarios and the 6 native lyric checks passed, and against the real audio engine the word fill never stepped backwards and stayed within about 6 ms of the engine's position. GitHub Actions built, tested and signed this installer on Windows. See the [validation report](https://github.com/SPARTANAC95/slate-music/blob/main/docs/VALIDATION.md).

Word times inside line-timed lyrics are an estimate, not a recording of the singer. Windows does not report the delay of every wireless device; that is what the Lyrics delay setting is for. Exclusive-mode output delay is calculated but was not measured on a physical DAC. Updater signatures are configured; a Windows Authenticode publisher certificate is not, so Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning. Opus, WMA, APE and protected files are unsupported.
